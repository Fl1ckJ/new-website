Blockchain & Data Integrity

Making trust verifiable.

Manufacturing records can pass through many organizations during the lifecycle of an asset. Establishing where information originated, who endorsed it and whether it has been changed is essential in safety-critical supply chains. SteelTrace uses blockchain technology as part of its trust infrastructure to provide an additional layer of verifiability, integrity and traceability for critical manufacturing information.

Blockchain Infrastructure

Cryptographic proofs, traceable history and digital signatures support the creation of trusted Smart Manufacturing Records with a verifiable audit trail across organizational boundaries.
